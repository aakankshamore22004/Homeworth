About the model

Area is not something you enter. A separate model predicts it from the other features, the same way the price model does.

Data
2,000 house records: 1,600 for training and 400 for testing.
Inputs
Bedrooms, bathrooms, stories, parking, six yes/no features and furnishing status.
Models
Two Random Forest regressors with 100 trees each, one for price and one for area.
Category
Under 1,000 sq.ft is small, under 2,000 is medium, larger is large.
